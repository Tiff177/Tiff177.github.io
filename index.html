Experience

I started working part-time in 2024 (after completing my DSE). Over the past two years, I have tried different types of jobs, including: food and beverage (fast-food restaurant, ramen shop), sales, promotion, and ticket distribution (travel agency). Even during the school term, I would work part-time whenever I had no classes (such as Saturdays and Sundays).

There are two main reasons I have kept working part-time. First, I hope to gain personal work experience by trying different occupations, learn various skills (e.g. communication skills, problem-solving skills, collaboration skills), and strengthen my personal abilities (e.g. stress tolerance, adaptability, time management).

Second, it allows me to be more independent. Although my income may not be much, I no longer need to ask my parents for money. Moreover, buying things I like with money I have earned myself gives me a great sense of achievement.

In addition, regular part-time work provides a relatively stable income, helping me increase my savings and develop good saving habits.
